I'm a final-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at [The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/chinese/index.html), advised by Prof. [Michael R. Lyu](http://www.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/lyu/home), and expect to graduate in July 2027. I received my Master's degree from Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen in June 2023, under the supervision of Prof. [Cuiyun Gao](https://cuiyungao.github.io/), and my Bachelor's degree from Harbin Institute of Technology, Weihai in June 2021.

My research focuses on **Code LLMs and Coding Agents**, spanning model training, evaluation, and inference-time optimization. More recently, I have been working on **recursive self-improvement**, particularly on enabling models to train themselves and optimize their own agent harnesses. I am currently a Research Intern at ByteDance Douyin AI4SE, working on long-horizon coding agents, agentic reinforcement learning, and automated post-training.
